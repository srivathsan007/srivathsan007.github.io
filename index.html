About

Architecture that scales securely

A decade of building, securing, and governing enterprise cloud infrastructure across global operations.

I architect and secure Microsoft 365, Azure, and AWS environments across multiple global tenants. My expertise spans SharePoint, Exchange, Entra ID, Conditional Access, and Zero Trust frameworks that protect organizations without slowing them down.

As an AI Security and Adoption Specialist, I safely roll out tools like Microsoft Copilot, Claude, Perplexity, ChatGPT, and custom MCP servers — ensuring every AI deployment has proper data governance through Purview DLP, sensitivity labels, DSPM, and Adaptive Protection.

Beyond architecture, I lead IT and cloud security functions — setting technical direction, building and mentoring teams, and owning security strategy with executive stakeholders. I specialize in email security architecture — gateway policies, threat detection, SSL certificate lifecycle — and lead enterprise-scale programmes including multi-tenant, multi-country migrations, RFP analysis, and vendor evaluations. I hold a Master's from Colorado State University and guest lecture on AI security topics.
